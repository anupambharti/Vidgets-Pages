1. Local by design

Vidgets for Android is an offline Home Screen widget and everyday-productivity app operated by Anupam Bharti. It has no account, ads, analytics, tracking SDK, purchases or developer backend. The Android app does not request Internet permission and does not transmit saved widget content to us.

2. Content on your device

Sets, notes, moments, priorities, focus history, manual habits, counters, current mood and preferences are stored in the app's private local files. Home Screen widgets and optional notifications display this content on your device. Anyone who can see those surfaces may see what you put there. Counters and habits are manual records, not health measurements. Mood is a latest selection, not medical advice.

3. Photos and permissions

The system photo/document picker lets you choose individual photos. Vidgets copies a resized image into private app storage and does not request access to your entire media library. Imported images are re-encoded without their original metadata. Timer notifications require permission on Android 13 and later and are optional. The app reschedules its existing timer after reboot or a time-zone/time change. It does not request exact-alarm, location, calendar, contacts, camera, microphone, advertising-ID or health permissions.

4. Sharing and export

Sharing only happens when you choose it. Android's share sheet lets you select the receiving app or person; their privacy practices apply. JSON exports exclude image files and cannot be imported, so they are not full backups. A set share can include your text only when you choose Include my content. We cannot recall copies you share.

5. Retention and deletion

Saved content remains until you delete an item or set, clear app storage, uninstall, or confirm Settings > Delete app data. That reset removes the local content, private photo copies, export cache and preferences. It cannot remove copies outside the app. Android cloud backup is disabled for this app; device manufacturers may have their own transfer or backup behavior.

6. Support and external websites

If you email admin@technoforbes.com, we receive what you choose to send, including your email address and message. We use support correspondence to respond and troubleshoot; avoid sending sensitive content you do not want to disclose. External links open a separate browser or email app. GitHub Pages hosts our public policy and support pages and may process technical request information such as IP addresses under GitHub's privacy statement. No website analytics, remote fonts or tracking scripts are added by us.

7. Children and changes

Vidgets is a general-purpose utility, not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal data through the app. Contact support with privacy questions. This policy should be reviewed when features, SDKs, hosting or data handling change. Updated 28 October 2026.
