Add your first widget

  1. Save content in Vidgets: choose a widget in Widgets, tap Customize, and save an item.
  2. Return to the Home Screen. Touch and hold an empty area and tap Widgets.
  3. Search for Vidgets. Touch and hold your chosen widget, drag it onto the Home Screen, and release.
  4. Choose a saved set and tap Save widget. Some widgets also let you pin one saved item.
  5. Resize using the handles if your launcher offers them. Launcher wording and supported sizes vary.

Vidgets also offers an Add to Home Screen request on supported launchers. Android asks you to confirm placement; the app cannot place widgets without your approval. The in-app four-step guide includes all 12 gallery names and a saved-content check.

Find or replay the tours

The first-launch welcome tour leads into a guided app tour with isolated practice controls. Reopen either from Settings: Guided app tour or Replay welcome tour. Settings also has Add widgets, step by step.

Choose a different set or item

Each installed widget keeps its own configuration. Touch and hold it and choose settings/edit if offered, or remove and add it again. Browsing a different set inside Vidgets does not change installed widgets. Quotes and Mood use app-wide preferences. A deleted set or pinned item shows an empty state until you reconfigure it.

Widget refresh and timer alerts

Open Vidgets to refresh saved content. Android and your launcher control periodic widget refresh timing. Timers use stored timestamps, so remaining time remains accurate across backgrounding and reopening. Paused time and breaks do not count toward Daily progress. Notifications are optional; Android may delay inexact completion alerts, especially during battery-saving modes. Check Settings in Vidgets and Android notification settings.

Manual habits, counters and mood

Habit check-ins use your chosen weekdays. Daily counters reset at the next local day; all-time counters keep their value. Last done supports logging and undo. Mood displays only your latest selection. Vidgets provides no medication tracking, diagnosis, treatment, health measurements or medical guidance.

Photos, sharing and your data

Choose individual photos with the system picker; no broad photo-library permission is required. Set sharing creates a style-only image, or an image with your text when you explicitly choose Include my content. Photo files are not included in set sharing or JSON exports. JSON is not a full backup and cannot be imported. Settings offers a confirmed Delete app data action. Deleting or uninstalling cannot recall externally shared copies.

Contact support

Email admin@technoforbes.com with your app version, device model, launcher name and steps to reproduce. Attach only content you are comfortable sharing. There is no account or developer cloud backup from which we can recover your local content.

Privacy

Read the Android privacy policy. This support page adds no tracking scripts, cookies, analytics or remote fonts.
